Our dataset is accessible through two methods.

Find and save data

Find and save data lets you create your own paginated reports. These can then be printed or exported for use in other software.

This option is best if you want to:

  • view an existing report
  • download a report as PDF, Excel or CSV
  • print a report
  • access detailed tabular data
  • produce information for meetings, audits or record keeping

With a free Power BI licence

You can:

  • open and view available paginated reports
  • export reports in supported formats
  • print reports

With a Power BI Pro licence

You can do everything available with a free licence. Depending on how your organisation has configured Power BI, you may also be able to share access to reports and collaborate with other users.

Explore this data

Explore this data lets you interact with the dataset and create your own view of the information.

This option is best if you want to:

  • answer a specific question using the data
  • create your own charts and tables
  • compare trends and patterns
  • build your own reports
  • create measures and calculations

With a free Power BI licence

You can:

  • explore and filter the data
  • create reports and visualisations
  • create measures and calculations
  • save reports to My Workspace
  • export data from visuals, where enabled

You cannot:

  • share reports with other users
  • save reports to a shared workspace
  • collaborate on reports with colleagues

With a Power BI Pro licence

You can do everything available with a free licence, and also:

  • save reports to shared workspaces
  • share reports with other users
  • collaborate with colleagues on reports and dashboards
